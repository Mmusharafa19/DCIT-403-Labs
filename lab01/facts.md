# Department of Computer Science: Office Facts (fictional)

1. The Department Office is in Room 104, ground floor of the Computer Science Block. It is open Monday to Friday, 8:30 am to 4:30 pm, and closed from 12:30 pm to 1:30 pm for lunch.
2. The Head of Department's office is Room 210, second floor. Appointments are booked through the Department Office, not directly.
3. The Undergraduate Programme Coordinator is Mrs. Ama Owusu (fictional), Room 106. She handles course registration problems, add/drop forms and transcript requests for the department. Email: ug.coordinator@cs.example.edu.gh.
4. The Computer Science Lab (CS Lab 1) is in Room 012, basement. It is open Monday to Saturday, 8:00 am to 8:00 pm. A valid student ID card is required to enter.
5. IT Support for student accounts and campus Wi-Fi is at the IT Help Desk, Room 015, basement, open Monday to Friday, 9:00 am to 5:00 pm. Email: helpdesk@cs.example.edu.gh.
6. Lecturers' office hours are posted on the notice board outside Room 104 every Monday morning.
7. Lost and found items in the Computer Science Block are kept at the Department Office (Room 104).
8. The Student Representative (Level 400) can be reached at csrep@students.example.edu.gh for student welfare issues.
