## Q1. What is the difference between an agent and a rational agent?

An **agent** is any entity that perceives its environment and takes actions in it.  
A **rational agent** is a special kind of agent that, given its percepts and any knowledge it has, chooses actions that maximize its expected performance (i.e., it behaves goal‑oriented and reasoned).  

So, all rational agents are agents, but not all agents are rational.

## Q2. Name the four parts of a PEAS description.

Performance measure, Environment, Actuators, Sensors.

## Q3. Why is a chatbot's environment usually partially observable?

A chatbot only “sees” the text it receives (the dialogue history). It has no direct access to the user’s internal state, external context, or the full world state that might influence the conversation. Because it can’t observe all relevant variables—just the messages it’s given—the environment is partially observable.
